Integration note

One application, one CV.

Apply and Auto Career Coach work together: the separate Coach workflow reads new applications from the shared sheet and emails results automatically. The original Apply workflow is unchanged. Your existing Apply workflow collects the CV and position, while the Coach workflow currently starts from its own form and requires a full job description. Making Coach run from the Apply submission needs a deliberate n8n connection and a source for that job description. This site does not silently modify the instructor's workflows or claim that the link is complete.
