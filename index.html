About the project

Automation that makes HR work simpler.

FlowSpace is a portfolio showcase of HR automation services. Its interactive explorer documents the Job Application workflow, while the service cards distinguish between available links and integrations that still need to be connected.

The original n8n workflow remains unchanged. The website is a presentation and access layer — not a replacement for the automation.

01
SubmitApplicants use the original n8n form to provide details and upload a PDF CV.
02
Validate & checkThe workflow checks submission validity and whether the same email applied for the same position.
03
StoreNew applications proceed to CV upload in Google Drive and a row in Google Sheets.
